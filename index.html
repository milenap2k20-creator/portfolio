02 — About

A little about me.

I’m a digital designer with a background in graphic design and a growing focus on UI/UX and game interfaces. I enjoy turning ideas into thoughtful visual experiences, combining functionality, atmosphere, and detail.

Outside of client work I draw, design prints, and build small interface experiments. I like problems where clarity and mood have to coexist — a HUD that reads in a split second, an app flow that feels calm. Lately I’ve been exploring motion, prototyping tools and new technologies that change how interfaces are made.
